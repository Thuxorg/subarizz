# Subarizz Technologies

**Built from struggle. Engineered for growth.**

Subarizz Technologies is a Tanzanian agri-food processing and small-scale manufacturing company. We design and manufacture practical potato peeling and chipping machines that help food vendors, households, restaurants, and cooperatives process more food with less physical strain.

## Our purpose

Across Tanzania, potato vendors often rely on hours of manual peeling with a knife. This work is repetitive, tiring, difficult to scale, and can expose workers to avoidable injury.

Subarizz exists to turn that daily struggle into opportunity. Our machines are locally engineered to make food preparation faster, safer, more hygienic, and more consistent—so entrepreneurs can serve more customers and build stronger businesses.

## What we build

### Potato Peeler

Our potato peeler is designed to process up to **20 kg of potatoes in one minute**. It reduces the burden of manual preparation while supporting consistent output for busy food businesses.

### Potato Chipper

Our potato chipper helps transform prepared potatoes into uniform chips efficiently, supporting better-quality products and smoother daily operations.
