01 / The origin

One kitchen. One problem. A new possibility.

Before there was a workshop, there was a son helping his mother peel potatoes by hand. Every late night became a question: what if hard work did not have to hurt?

CHAPTER 01 — THE BURDEN

Hours of effort, held in a blade.

Manual peeling kept food vendors crouched over buckets for long days—limiting output, placing strain on hands, and leaving little room for growth.

CHAPTER 02 — THE SKETCH

An answer started on the workbench.

Night after night, ideas became drawings: a tougher, repairable machine built around the reality of a small food business.

CHAPTER 03 — THE MACHINE

Built here. Made for what’s next.

Today, Subarizz turns practical engineering into dignity, speed, and opportunity for people feeding their communities every day.
